Privacy policy

openclaw-gog is a personal tool used only by its owner, to access the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the tool accesses Gmail and Google Calendar data of the owner's own account.

How it is used

The data is used only to summarize the owner's emails and calendar and to create calendar events or send emails when the owner asks. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. Email and calendar content is not stored outside the owner's devices, except as processed by the owner's chosen AI assistant to produce summaries.
